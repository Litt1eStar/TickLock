How to play

Hit the zone

Press Space while the needle is inside a gold zone. Every hit turns the needle around. Hit the white centre for a perfect, worth double. Blue zones add 2 seconds.

Miss, then save it

A near miss costs 0.4 seconds. A full miss costs 1.2 seconds and opens a green recovery zone ahead. Hit it to win the time back and keep half your combo.

Hold Shift to sweep 1.8 times faster for double points. Ten hits in a row starts flow, worth 1.25 times. The 30-second clock waits for your first hit.
